# Weekend partner TV slide

**Mode:** Persuade (silent bar TV in a partner rotation)

**Audience & job.** Someone already in a downtown Spearfish bar this weekend glances at a TV from across the room. They need to know the Invitational is happening now, where the daytime rounds are, what the evening shows are, and how to get the full guide.

**Action.** Scan the QR to qcsongwriters.com.

**Proof / content.** Real 2026 schedule from the digital booklet: Fri/Sat 11 A.M.–4 P.M. venue rotation across Wine Bar / False Bottom / Public House; Friday Songwriters Showcase (free) and Saturday Jeffrey Foucault (tickets) at The Matthews; 18 songwriters.

**Constraints.** Fixed 1920×1080; readable at bar distance; one slide, no interaction beyond QR; generic partner (not venue-branded); runs Fri Sept 25 and Sat Sept 26, 2026; Field Rule (floor + paper QR sheet); no second accent; no radius.

**Direction.** The weekend as a running order — two day columns of time·venue rows, evening strips on tape, paper QR sheet. Established Running Order world; shaped directly from a precise brief.

**Memorable moment.** Spike-tape evening callouts next to the Fri/Sat split, with the paper QR sheet as the only field change.
